Privacy policy

How Narris International handles the personal information you share with us. Discretion is part of how we work.

Draft for legal review. Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

Narris International is a strategy advisory based in Nairobi, Kenya, serving clients worldwide. For this policy we act as the data controller of information collected through this website. Contact: .

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the contact form, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp: your name, organisation, role, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and your message. We do not ask for sensitive personal data. Please do not include it in your message.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation if you ask for one, and manage the resulting relationship. Our lawful basis is your consent and, where you have asked us to respond, steps taken at your request. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Only the Narris team. Form submissions pass through a form-handling service that delivers them to us by email. If a message is sent from a client outside Kenya, or through a provider outside Kenya, it may be processed abroad. We take reasonable steps to keep such transfers in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purpose above, or as the law requires. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted after a reasonable period.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to access your personal data, correct it, have it deleted, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time. Write to us at the address above. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Cookies and storage

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one small item in your browser to remember that you have seen the cookie notice.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
